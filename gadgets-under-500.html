Top 5 Gadgets Under ₹500

Finding useful gadgets on a student budget can be difficult. Fortunately, there are many affordable tech accessories available that improve productivity and everyday convenience without costing a fortune. Here are five useful gadgets that are often available for under ₹500.

1. Mobile Phone Stand

A mobile stand is a simple but useful accessory for online classes, watching videos, video calls, and studying. It keeps your phone stable and allows hands-free usage.

2. LED USB Light

A USB-powered LED light can be connected directly to a laptop or power bank. It is useful for studying at night or working in low-light conditions.

3. Cable Organizer

Cable organizers help keep charging cables, earphones, and USB cords neat and untangled. They improve desk organization and reduce cable clutter.

4. Cleaning Kit for Electronics

Dust and fingerprints can affect screens and keyboards. A budget cleaning kit helps keep laptops, monitors, phones, and keyboards clean and looking new.

5. USB Hub

Many modern laptops have limited ports. A basic USB hub allows users to connect multiple devices such as pen drives, keyboards, and mice at once.

Conclusion

Affordable gadgets do not need to be expensive to be useful. A phone stand, LED USB light, cable organizer, cleaning kit, and USB hub can improve productivity and convenience while staying within a budget.
